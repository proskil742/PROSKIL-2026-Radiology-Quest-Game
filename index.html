What is a Diagnostic Radiographer?

Radiographers are certified medical imaging healthcare professionals who combine human anatomy, physics, and advanced technology to create diagnostic images inside the human body.

1. Radiation Protection & ALARA

They follow the ALARA principle (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) using lead aprons and targeted collimation to protect patients.

2. Patient Safety & Comfort

They ensure zero ferromagnetic metals enter MRI suites, assist trauma emergency cases, and comfort anxious patients.

3. World Radiography Day

Celebrated annually on November 8th marking Wilhelm Röntgen's discovery of X-Rays in 1895!
